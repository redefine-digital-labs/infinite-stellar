# Delivery Milestones and Season Operations

## Confirmed delivery objective

Deliver a visually polished, genuinely multiplayer Sui-native Dark Forest-style
game in the existing Infinite Stellar repository. The owner confirmed sustained
delivery through these milestones. Local simulation and presentation increments
do not complete the full objective.

| Milestone | Deliverable | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Tactical presentation | Legible planets, resources, routes, responsive floating controls, pan/zoom/Home/Fit | No blocked essential controls or route misalignment; desktop and mobile checks |
| 2. Complete player journey | Soul entry, home, exploration, combat, upgrades, artifacts, Rips, recovery | A new player completes exploration and conquest; failures explain the next action |
| 3. Shared chain season | Proof-backed actions, wallet transactions, shared state and finality recovery | Two independent wallets establish homes, fight, and reconnect to consistent onchain outcomes |
| 4. Production operations | Vault export/restore, public indexing, monitoring, bounded sponsorship, load and outage recovery | Recovery and contention rehearsals pass with documented operational limits |
| 5. Audit and release | Independent audit remediation, real multisig custody, verified mainnet and web deployment | Reviewed artifacts and approvals match deployed bytes; players can join the real season |

## Proposed cadence, pending playtest evidence

These are product recommendations, not deployed constants, measured capacity,
or an instruction to change the existing Round 5 parity ruleset.

| Stage | Competitive duration | Enrolled Seat cap per universe | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guided tutorial | 15–30 minutes | One local learner | Learn without spending or risking a ranked position |
| Technical rehearsal | 24–48 hours | 8–16 invited controllers | Validate real proofs, contention, finality, and recovery |
| First player pilot | 72 hours | 32 | Observe encounters, pacing, sleep burden, and support needs |
| Standard season | 7 days | Start at 64; expand to 128 after evidence | Allow exploration, diplomacy, combat, and a meaningful finish |

Caps count enrolled Seats, not simultaneously connected browsers. Do not
describe 128 as tested Sui capacity. Stress-test shared-Planet contention,
arrival queues, prover devices, RPC limits, and sponsor cost before increasing
the cap. Keep one populated universe initially; split populations only when
demand and retention support it. Choose world size and spawn distribution
before revealing the seed; never move players or resize an active season to
manufacture encounters.

The pilot's 72 hours refer to competitive play. Registration, seed observation,
home establishment, and bounded final settlement have separately published
windows. Never shorten home availability or drop pending arrivals to fit a
marketing duration. If verified turnout before competitive opening is below
the announced minimum, use the declared cancellation/re-enrollment policy;
never silently change the schedule or manufacture participant counts.

The official [DF Round 5 announcement](https://blog.zkga.me/v6-r5-announce)
specified a ten-day round and a four-hour holding interval for capture scoring.
Shorter Infinite Stellar rounds therefore require explicit, versioned pacing
configuration and playtests. Freeze growth, voyage timing, capture intervals,
artifact cooldowns, home/recovery windows, and endgame deadlines together.
Keep the original parity configuration available as the behavioral reference.

Start standard operations with one seven-day competitive season every two
weeks. Use the intervening week for settlement review, fixes, onboarding, and
the next enrollment. Announce exact UTC boundaries and show local time in the
client. No automated opening or recurring paid infrastructure is authorized by
this proposal alone.

## Player time and fairness

Aim for useful 15–30 minute visits, two or three times a day, outside chosen
competitive sessions. This is a design target, not an offline-protection
guarantee: asynchronous fleets and opponents continue while a player is away.
Measure overnight losses and participation across time zones. Do not promise
safe sleep until a published, tested ruleset actually provides that behavior.

The default reference client should explain incoming attacks and offer opt-in
notifications without sending private map preimages to telemetry. A shared
schedule cannot privilege one geography indefinitely; announce future season
opening rotations in advance. Client scripts cannot enforce human-only play.
Publish permitted automation and sponsor limits explicitly; address/Soul Seat
limits are not proof of one human per account.

## Operating loop

1. Publish the season rules, capacity, eligibility, schedule, artifact policy,
   sponsor budget and exhaustion behavior, scoring, and incident rules.
2. Open advance enrollment and provide the local tutorial. Verify Soul access
   and backup readiness; never assume all interested players already own a Soul.
3. Run staffed launch support, monitor home establishment, proofs, chain
   finality, index lag, sponsor consumption, and privacy-safe failure reasons.
4. Publish public-state progress and player-approved stories. Never reveal
   private coordinates, witnesses, or exports in rankings or community posts.
5. Close actions at the frozen boundaries, resolve eligible arrivals, settle,
   and publish verifiable results before the next season.
6. Compare activation, meaningful interaction, next-season return, proof latency,
   technical failures, support load, and sponsor cost per active Seat. Change
   balance only between seasons.

Initial ranked play should have no entry fee, token sale, paid combat power, or
promised cash prizes. Any Gas sponsorship must have a funded total budget and
per-controller/action limits; budget exhaustion must be visible. These are
recommendations, not authorization to spend money or launch a campaign.

Recognition can include participation, exploration, combat, and community
contributions. Only validated receipts may support official achievements.
Soul history and cosmetic expression may persist; planets, fleet strength,
resources, and ranked advantage reset. Soul memory writes require the supported
holder-approved flow. External artifact extraction must follow the published
custody and next-season eligibility rules; it cannot introduce paid or inherited
ranked power.

## Current implementation boundary

Visual improvements are in progress. The public website currently supports the
local simulation; mainnet gameplay, independent audit clearance, production
proof material, and an approved multisig release remain unfinished. This plan
does not claim that the proposed schedule, service capacity, or rewards are live.
